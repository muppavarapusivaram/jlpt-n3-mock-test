# JLPT N3 Question Bank: Difficulty Rating Guidelines

This document outlines the standard 1–5 difficulty rating system used across the JLPT N3 question bank.

## Scale Definitions

### Level 1: Very Easy (N4/N3 Bridge)
- Straightforward, single-concept grammar or vocabulary.
- Direct textbook forms with obvious context clues.
- Distractors are clearly ungrammatical or from much lower levels.
- Example: Basic て-form contractions (`〜といて`, `〜ちゃった`) in simple imperative sentences.

### Level 2: Easy (Standard Elementary N3)
- Direct application of a single core N3 grammar point.
- Context makes the semantic intent unambiguous (e.g., clear purpose with `〜ように`).
- Standard conjugations with minimal irregular forms.

### Level 3: Medium (Target Official JLPT N3 Standard)
- Standard difficulty matching the median JLPT N3 official exam question.
- Distinguishes between closely related expressions (e.g., `〜みたい` vs `〜らしい` vs `〜っぽい`, `〜ように` vs `〜ために`).
- Requires understanding sentence mood, perspective (giver/receiver), or speaker intent.
- Standard starred ordering questions (文の組み立て) with standard syntactic structure.

### Level 4: Hard (Advanced N3)
- Combines multiple grammatical markers (e.g., causative-passive + request `〜させてもらえませんか`, or suffering passive `〜にいられると迷惑`).
- Subtly deceptive distractors that sound colloquially natural but are grammatically incorrect in context.
- Starred sentence ordering questions with complex embedded clauses or particle-heavy predicates.
- Text grammar (文章の文法) questions requiring cohesive reading across multiple sentences or paragraphs.

### Level 5: Very Hard (N3 Challenge / N2 Horizon)
- Intricate discourse-level connectors or rare communicative nuances.
- Starred questions where word order variations look equally plausible without strict syntactic analysis.
- Passages with subtle tense/aspect shifts or implicit subject omissions across paragraphs.

---

## Tagging Protocol
- Every question must be assigned an integer difficulty between 1 and 5.
- Target distribution across a full mock test:
  - Difficulty 1: ~10%
  - Difficulty 2: ~25%
  - Difficulty 3: ~40%
  - Difficulty 4: ~20%
  - Difficulty 5: ~5%
